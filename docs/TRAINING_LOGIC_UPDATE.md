# Training logic update

## Ordinary scenario training
- Evaluation is scoped to the selected training objective only.
- Focused objection scenarios evaluate only the manager's handling of that objection.
- Score: 1–100: 90–100 excellent, 80–89 good, 60–79 satisfactory, 41–59 poor, 1–40 very poor.

## Free training
- Separate mode with no fixed objection.
- AI may introduce different objections, but not on every message.
- Objections depend on the dialogue and prior manager answer.
- When the manager performs strongly across need discovery, product/value presentation, communication and objection handling, the client moves to purchase.
- Successful completion phrase: `Хорошо, я готов купить у вас товар. Высылайте счёт.`

## Cold call
- Keep cold-call evaluation separate from ordinary training evaluation and preserve its 5×20 model.
